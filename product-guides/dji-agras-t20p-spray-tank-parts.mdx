## Use four identifiers together

1. **Aircraft model:** confirm that the part is for the DJI Agras T20P.
2. **Diagram item number:** locate the numbered component in the product diagram.
3. **Material number:** compare the full code character by character.
4. **Component name and context:** confirm the part's function and the adjacent assembly before ordering.

## Pre-order checklist

- Record the aircraft model and serial or configuration information used by your maintenance process.
- Photograph the installed component and its surrounding connections before disassembly.
- Compare the complete material number; do not match only the last digits.
- Confirm whether seals, fasteners, cables, brackets, or related modules are included or ordered separately.
- Check current availability and the selected variant on the live product page.
- Ask UNITED UAV for clarification if the diagram, code, component name, or required quantity does not agree.

## Installation and return-to-service checks

Power off the aircraft and remove the battery before inspection or replacement work. Inspect sealing surfaces, cable routing, connectors, clips, brackets, hoses, and mounting hardware as applicable. After installation, verify alignment, cable clearance, secure fasteners, leak-free fluid connections, and correct sensor or module behavior before field use.

Installation should be completed by a qualified technician familiar with DJI Agras T20P maintenance procedures. Follow the latest manufacturer documentation and applicable safety requirements.
